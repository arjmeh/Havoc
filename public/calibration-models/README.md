# Calibration model exports

Browser-ready exports generated by
`source-assets/calibration-3d/generate_calibration_kit.py`.

- `glass-tumbler.glb` and its three transparent WebP sprite views define the
  glass used during the camera pullback and infinite field.
- `ice-face-cube-shell.glb` and its sprite views are designed to layer over the
  live/captured portrait.
- `ice-cube-field.glb` and its sprite match the face cube’s outer dimensions
  for physically consistent falling ice.
- `manifest.json` records sprite routes, liquid bounds, portrait opening, and
  provenance.

Lossless PNG render masters live with the editable source under
`source-assets/calibration-3d/renders/`; the public WebPs use quality 92 with
lossless alpha. All geometry and materials are original Havoc project assets
produced locally with the free Blender 5.2 LTS build.
